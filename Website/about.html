About This Website

Fortnite: Save the Island is a website about looking after the environment. It uses a Fortnite-inspired theme to make environmental information more interesting for students

The website focuses on everyday problems such as rubbish, pollution, damage to natural areas and the harm these can cause to wildlife

The aim is to help visitors understand these issues and find simple actions they can take at home, at school or in their local community

What Is Kaitiakitanga?

Kaitiakitanga is about caring for and protecting the environment. It includes looking after the land, water, plants and animals around us

It also means thinking about how our choices affect nature and the people who will live here in the future

We can practise kaitiakitanga by reducing waste, respecting wildlife, keeping waterways clean and looking after shared spaces

Why Does It Matter?

Rubbish and pollution can damage habitats and harm wildlife. When natural areas are not looked after, animals and plants can lose the places they need to survive

People depend on a healthy environment too. Clean water, healthy ecosystems and natural spaces are important for our communities

You do not need to fix every problem on your own. Starting with small actions and encouraging others to help is a good way to make a difference
